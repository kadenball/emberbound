# Full-game quality target — open

The user wants a funny, polished, challenging Android/iOS action adventure with the completeness and polish of Castle Crashers or Cuphead, the readable challenge of Super Meat Boy, and an original grotesque toy/cartoon identity. Version numbers and green tests do not redefine that target. The goal remains open.

Near-term priorities should deepen the adventure rather than add more menu labels: bespoke encounter transitions and player-caused comic consequences, deeper boss performance and clearer arena mechanics, then direct human review of movement, feedback, music and challenge. Preserve reward correctness and hazard readability while making those improvements.
