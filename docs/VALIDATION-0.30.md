The user requested a soundtrack replacement before the 0.30 download package was finalized. The final 0.31 package includes this pass and its later music changes. No 0.30 native-emulator success is claimed.

- 244 simulation tests pass across 27 files. All nine hero/style campaigns finish with earned progression and up to three entry retries per chapter attempt, plus at most three whole-chapter attempts using capped loss payouts. The co-op probe also allows three actual entry retries. No health edits or skipped waves are used by campaign probes.
- The broad browser run passed 214 checks, skipped 14 inapplicable cases and failed six cases: three old `Fight 3 / 6` expectations, and three brute tests that required winning the entire now-longer chapter after the specific brute encounter. The save tests now expect nine encounters. The brute test now stops after the first brute encounter is cleared, while still requiring the observed pound and survival. Full-campaign viability is tested separately.
- After fixing those test assumptions and a final sprite-clipping correction, all 50 applicable focused browser checks passed; four host-specific cases were skipped. This rerun covered hero art, real attack controls, blood, bookmarks/storage, brute stance/combos and menu autoplay on desktop Chromium, Android-sized Chromium and iPhone-sized WebKit. The entire broad suite was not rerun after the clipping-only renderer change.

The counter-only intermediate build reduced this to 4/108 and zero boss chapters while mixed stayed at 103/108. Final expanded-route rapid and planted-in-reach Heavy policies each win 0/108 with matched old and freshly earned profiles. Mixed wins 98/108 with matched profiles and 100/108 with fresh profiles. Every evaluation terminates without retries. [Detailed audit links and limitations](UPDATE-0.30.md).

Phone-sized full-renderer fixtures and actual-input blood captures were also produced. These are rendering evidence, not human animation or fun approval.
