The crowd-healing regression explicitly checks a six-HP earned heal. All nine hero/style campaign probes still complete with earned progression and validate encounter bookmarks.

New defense tests first cause a real blocked Heavy, then use mixed inputs to damage an active boss through its head/rear opening. The initial wafer capture showed the hero obscured by the boss at close range; a small boss rendering depth allowance fixed that obstruction, and the new final capture was inspected. Collision is unchanged. Captures are listed in [0.14](UPDATE-0.14.md).

Snackula Heavy-only wins fell 9 → 3 and Frank 9 → 6 out of nine each. Mixed play won 107/108 chapters and 54/54 bosses. The mixed policy was taught the new visible counters, so these are not identical input recordings or human enjoyment evidence. See [methodology and raw results](UPDATE-0.14.md).

- Android `assembleDebug bundleRelease lintRelease` passed on the final assets, **version 0.14.0/code 14**. The debug APK is installable; the release AAB remains unsigned. The existing dependency lint baseline remains.
- Xcode project syntax and two iOS plists parse. Native iOS build/signing has not been verified on this Linux host.
- Packaging compares every web/audio asset in both native packages against the current production build before collecting downloads and source checksums.
