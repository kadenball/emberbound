# 0.8 — A world with terrible habits

The old maps repeated small toy scenery along a long road. This pass adds oversized ruined household landmarks, deeper shadows, mould, leaking machinery, discarded stuffing, torn signs and distinct bridge materials. Six regions now center on a rotting picnic kitchen, freezer mortuary, laundry outfall, tooth-filled confectionery, workplace press and cardboard tax palace. Building acts vary props, roof details and signs. Static scenery remains chunk-cached; mobile memory does not grow with chapter length.

Machinery now participates in three selected encounters per journey and one boss-approach encounter. Every machine starts with a full non-damaging lead-in, gives a 1.4-second warning, attacks for one second, and cools down. A player can take at most one successful machine hit per cycle. Normal damage inherits the existing regional scaling. Machines damage ordinary enemies as well as players. Bonk the separate shutdown lever to disable one for the rest of the attempt.

- **Arse-spore:** jump a low spore eruption or move around it.
- **Freezer Burn:** the cutter sweeps across its marked corridor during the active window.
- **The Suckhole:** the warning gently pulls grounded players inward; jumping and dodging escape the pull.
- **Chewing You:** warning syrup slows grounded movement; jumping or dodging escapes before it erupts.
- **Staff Flattener / Death & Taxes:** overhead stamps require a lane change; jumping alone does not avoid them. These share a press behavior, with different regional art.

These hazards give players a reason to bait, reposition and use the environment. They are not six wholly unrelated subsystems. No generic enemy HP increase was added in this pass. Dead creatures leave a bounded trail of stuffing and tooth scraps for the rest of the run.

A prologue, chapter introductions and boss aftermaths connect the six crown teeth to a royal stuffing-tax scam and a suspicious buffet pass. Seen scenes stay skipped on retries; the campaign map can replay introductions. Boss rewards bank before their aftermath scene. Bangers headings, speech panels and creature portraits give the UI a stronger comic identity.

Twelve original arranged chapter recordings replace the short musical sequencer. Editable MIDI and composition sources ship with the project; external instrument/font licenses are retained. The user-selected Udio recording remains the menu theme, and menu/chapter transport obeys Music settings and pause. Music decoding is verified; human listening and mix approval remain outstanding.

The comparison with Super Meat Boy and Castle Crashers is recorded in [LEVEL-DESIGN-RESEARCH.md](LEVEL-DESIGN-RESEARCH.md). This pass addresses coherent environments and meaningful hazards. Fast mid-chapter retries, more elaborate player-caused comedy, bespoke encounter transitions and external play review remain further work. It does not establish parity with either game's quality.
